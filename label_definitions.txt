# Every label answers one question: what is the headline's main point?

business: the headline's point is a deliberate move by a company or person: a launch, layoff, deal, product, or plan
incident: the headline's point is something that went wrong: a hack, attack, breach, outage, accident, or AI misbehaving
legal: the headline's point is government, courts, regulators, or police acting: a lawsuit, ruling, ban, fine, arrest, or bill
claim: the headline's point is an opinion, warning, prediction, or demand, from a named person, an organization, or the writer
analysis: the headline's point is something measured: a number, study, or data result
